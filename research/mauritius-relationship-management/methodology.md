# Methodology

## Research Question

What relationship-management and adjacent operational problems are repeatedly
reported by Mauritian employees, customers, businesses, conglomerates,
regulators, and public institutions?

## Source Priority

1. National surveys and official statistics.
2. Regulators, ombudspersons, government architecture, and audit reports.
3. Integrated and annual reports from major Mauritian organizations.
4. Established Mauritian business and news publications.
5. Social posts and forums for language and scenario discovery only.

## Interpretation

Repeated findings across surveys, regulator cases, audits, and independent
organizations are treated as strong signals.

Company reports are useful for identifying operating priorities and admitted
constraints, but they are self-reported.

News coverage provides timely examples and quotations but may emphasize unusual
events.

Social posts are anecdotal. They are not used to estimate prevalence or rank
problems unless supported by stronger evidence.

## Limitations

- Some reports describe 2023 or 2024 data despite later publication dates.
- Search engines do not reliably index Facebook and private community posts.
- Several reports provide aggregate rather than record-level data.
- The research identifies recurring problem classes, not the requirements of a
  specific future customer.
- Product implementation must validate terminology, permissions, SOPs, and
  measures with the selected organization.

## Evidence Handling

Committed findings paraphrase source material and use short excerpts only when
necessary. The source register records URLs and local filenames. Downloaded
source documents remain ignored unless their redistribution terms are
confirmed.
